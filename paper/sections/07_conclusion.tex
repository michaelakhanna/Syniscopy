\section{Conclusion}

Syniscopy's useful claim is narrow and testable: when several nanoparticle-imaging modalities are expressed on the same scene parameters, their Fisher diagnostics, fusion algebra, and supervision labels can be generated with explicit status and provenance. The revised workflow makes the derivative gates, detected-quanta normalization, source-use separation, and SAM2 worked-example scope visible in generated artifacts rather than in defensive prose. The result is not a universal modality ranking; it is a reproducible way to say exactly what comparison was made, which assumptions supported it, and which rows should not be promoted beyond diagnostics.
